Shopping needs, stock and visits are different records. Read inventory_products/stock and shopping_list before creating items; reuse product IDs. For one-offs use unlinkedReason. Use household stock units (1 jar is 1); don't invent weights.

Shopping lists: stockNeed "out" = below minimum or essential at minimum; "nearly_out" = nonessential at minimum. Bought credits known stock once, never twice. "Not now" skips without changing stock; reopen when needed. Preserve minimum rules and recipe needs.

Shopping visits are OPTIONAL suggestions, not a required checklist. Start for a shop/group or chosen requirements; add any catalogue product or one-off. Before buying, substitute ANY catalogue product without globally linking shared stock. After partial purchase, only shared-stock variants can replace a line. purchasedQuantity is the TOTAL bought, not an increment. Update with latest item revision and stable operationId; reread on conflict. Closing with open suggestions is allowed; deleting an ACTIVE visit hides it but keeps needs, real purchases, stock changes, payments and receipts.

Products may sell at several shops. The actual receipt shop supersedes a planned shop for matched acquisitions. Receipts are optional: upload directly with shopping_session_upload_receipt (base64 PDF/image under 4 MB) or attach an existing document; match receipt lines with stable references and reuse prior acquisitions, including purchases before the visit. Review quantity/count differences; never double-credit stock.

Payments: get shopping_payment_options first; record each actual payment once with a stable reference, correct payer and account. Receipts alone create no expense.
