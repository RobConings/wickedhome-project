- Stock = at home; shopping_list = needs; shopping_session = visit; purchases = goods. Reuse ids: inventory_products → inventory_stock → shopping_list; recipes_plan. One-offs need unlinkedReason.
- 1 jar = 1; no required grams. Use known stock units; containers differ.
- stockNeed: below minimum OR essential at minimum = out/Out/to_buy; nonessential at minimum = nearly_out/Nearly out/warning. The person decides what to buy; filter stockNeed, preserve minima. skipped = Not now; open restores. Bought credits known stock once; never add twice. Deletes ask; children add/tick/remove own rows.
- shopping_start_session: selection matching + one shopId OR shopGroupId creates the current checklist; Explicit requirementIds use selection selected. Reuse operationId on retry. includeLowStock false excludes automatic quantities. shopping_sessions → shopping_session; update latest ITEM revision. purchasedQuantity is TOTAL, not extra. Reuse id/payload on retry; reread conflicts. Adjust quantity/productId buys nothing; only linked variants sharing stock. Prior purchases unchanged. Extras: session_add_item. Close after open rows resolved.
- Receipt: upload → shopping_session_attach_receipt → shopping_session_match_receipt, stable line. Link prior purchases; ask differences/counts. Add missing products also after closing; never add stock twice.
- Payment: shopping_payment_options for expense/account/payer ids; shopping_session_record_payment, stable reference per payment. Never infer paid totals from stock or duplicate expenses.

- Shopping visits are optional suggestions, not compulsory checklists. A visit may finish with OPEN items unchanged, and an ACTIVE visit can be removed via shopping_delete_session (current revision). Deleting hides only the visit; stock purchases, payments, document receipts and ordinary needs survive. Do not delete closed visits.
- Before the first acquisition on a row, shopping_session_update_item productId can pick ANY catalogue product as substitute, not only globally shared-stock alternatives. After a partial purchase, only shared-stock variants can replace it without changing past goods. shopping_session_add_item accepts a catalogue product OR free-text name, so users need not create/link a product first; the app can also create one directly during the visit.
- An item may be sold at more than one shop: preserve its shop links and use the real receipt's shop for confirmed acquisitions. Never reject a purchase solely because the planned/old retailer differs. Previously recorded acquisitions may be selected for matching even when they predate sessions.
- A receipt is optional. When supplied, shopping_session_upload_receipt accepts complete base64 PDF/JPEG/PNG/WebP file bytes (max 4 MB) with printed date, then attaches it; ordinary purchase flows do not require a document. shopping_session_attach_receipt remains available for documents already uploaded.
