- Cooking: recipes_list → match/save links → recipes_plan (default missing); never copy ingredients manually.

- Bought → shopping_update_item: known stock credited once. status skipped = Nu niet; open restores. Check acquisitions before adding stock again. Deletes ask; children add/tick/remove own rows, adults manage.
- Visits: shopping_plan_visit rechecks needs; shopping_start_session selects only chosen requirementIds, with shopIds/shopGroupId. Read shopping_session for ids/revisions; update with stable operationId. Buy partial totals or skip; close only after resolving open items.
- Visit receipts: shopping_session_attach_receipt then shopping_session_match_receipt; match prior goods, ask about differences/counts. Payments are separate: shopping_session_record_payment links an expense or records a confirmed paid amount with a stable reference. Never infer paid totals from stock.
