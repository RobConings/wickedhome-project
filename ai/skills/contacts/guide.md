- A contact is anyone the household deals with: contractor, plumber, electrician, carpenter, supplier, the bank, the school, the doctor… Name, company, trade, emails, phones, address, website, notes. The first email and phone are the primary ones.
- Contacts work on projects with a role ("contractor" on "Bathroom renovation"). Project contacts are linked here, not in the projects skill.
- Refer to a contact by name, company or trade: "Jan", "Bouwbedrijf Peeters", "the contractor", "the plumber", "the bathroom contractor". Trades match in Dutch and English. Several fit → the one on a running project, otherwise ask which one.
- "What is the plumber's phone number?" → `contacts_find` contact "plumber" (or query "plumber" for a list).
- "Who is working on the bathroom?" → `contacts_find` project "bathroom".
- "Add Jan Peeters, contractor at Peeters Construction, jan@example.be" → `contacts_save` name, trade, company, email. Add project + role to link it at once.
- New number or address for someone → `contacts_save` contact + phone/email (becomes the primary one; the others stay).
- "Peeters is the contractor for the kitchen" → `contacts_link` contact, project, role "contractor"; unlink true to remove.
- Delete only when asked (`contacts_delete`); project links go with it, projects stay.
- Mailing or calling is not done here: give the email or number, or use the mail skill with the contact.
- Everyone may look; changes are for owners and adults and always confirmed.
