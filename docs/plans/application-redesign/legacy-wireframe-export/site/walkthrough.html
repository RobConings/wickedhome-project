Connected home

Your home starts with its rooms

The home dashboard keeps the house map prominent. Choose a floor, tap a room or cycle through rooms without leaving Home.

  1. Uses the existing floor-plan viewer and saved room outlines.
  2. Appliances appear at their real saved positions, with device state and freshness owned by Devices.
  3. Room work markers open the room context: appliances, open tasks and current projects.
  4. Rooms without an outline remain selectable; unavailable information is shown as unknown.
What happens nextSelect another room, open a linked task or project, or open the room itself.

Places owns rooms and geometry. Devices, Energy, Tasks and Projects contribute their existing records.

Projects and money

One project, with its useful connections

The project answers three questions: what is next, what do we need, and how is the budget doing?

  1. The existing project cover banner stays: the image helps identify the project immediately.
  2. A stable header and related-record cards replace unrelated blocks and loose links.
  3. Related records use coloured icons, counts and a whole-card tap target; phones show two cards per row.
  4. Real task progress, residents and a clear next step make the project feel achievable.
  5. Spent, still planned and remaining amounts have separate meanings.
What happens nextOpen Materials to see what the task needs, then open the product.

Projects hosts the page; Tasks, Shopping, Inventory and Expenses provide their own records.

A product keeps one identity throughout the household

Shopping

Start a trip by choosing one shop or saved group

No trip-name form, preparation wizard or second list of shop checkboxes.

  1. The shared selection control puts favourites and recent choices first.
  2. A saved group already defines its shops; users do not select them again.
  3. The matching shopping list becomes the checklist automatically.

Shops owns destinations and groups. Shopping reuses existing needs.
